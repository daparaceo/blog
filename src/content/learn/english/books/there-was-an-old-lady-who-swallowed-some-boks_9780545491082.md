<p><b>Introduction</b></p>
<p style="text-indent: 1em;"><i>There Was an Old Lady Who Swallowed Some Boks</i> by Lucille Colandro is a funny rhyming story from the <i>There Was an Old Lady</i> series. The old lady swallows many holiday items, and each new item connects to the one before it.</p>

<p><b>Body</b></p>
<p style="text-indent: 1em;">I liked the repeating pattern because it made the story easy and fun to read aloud. Words like <i>jingled</i>, <i>jangled</i>, and <i>velvety</i> helped me imagine the sounds and textures in the story. The silly idea of swallowing a bell, bows, gifts, and even a sleigh made the book very playful.</p>

<p><b>Conclusion</b></p>
<p style="text-indent: 1em;">This book is a good choice for young English readers who enjoy rhyme, repetition, and funny surprises. I would recommend it to readers who want to practice reading with rhythm.</p>
