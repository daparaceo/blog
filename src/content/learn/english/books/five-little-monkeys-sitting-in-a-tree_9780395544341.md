<div class="review-en">
<p><b>Introduction</b><br>
"Five Little Monkeys Sitting in a Tree" by Eileen Christelow is a playful picture book that follows five mischievous monkeys who tease a sleepy crocodile while their mother naps nearby.</p>
<p><b>Body</b><br>
What I loved most about this book is its rhythmic, repetitive text. Each time a monkey disappears with a "SNAP!", young readers feel a little jolt of suspense, only to be relieved when the monkey turns out to be safe. The counting pattern from five down to one, and back up to five, makes the story perfect for practicing numbers while enjoying a fun narrative. The illustrations add humor and warmth, especially in the final scene where the mama monkey hugs and scolds her little ones.</p>
<p><b>Conclusion</b><br>
This book is a wonderful choice for early readers who enjoy predictable, rhyming stories with a gentle lesson about safety. It's engaging, funny, and easy to read aloud together.</p>
