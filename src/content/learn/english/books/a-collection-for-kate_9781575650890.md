<p><b>Introduction</b></p>
<p>A collection for Kate is a story by Barbara DeRubertis. It is about a girl named Kate who has to share a collection at school.</p>
<p><b>Body</b></p>
<p>Kate feels worried because her classmates have many books, magnets, shells, postcards, and pigs. As she watches them, she adds the numbers and thinks her own things are not enough.</p>
<p><b>Conclusion</b></p>
<p>I liked this book because Kate solves her problem in a clever way. The story also helps readers practice addition while enjoying a school story.</p>
