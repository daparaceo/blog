<p><b>Introduction</b></p>
<p style="text-indent: 1em;"><i>George and Martha</i> by James Marshall is a funny and warm book about two hippo friends. The book has five short stories, and each story shows a different part of friendship.</p>
<p><b>Body</b></p>
<p style="text-indent: 1em;">I liked how George and Martha sometimes make mistakes but still care about each other. George does not want to hurt Martha's feelings, and Martha teaches him that friends should tell the truth. The stories also show that friends need privacy, kindness, and encouragement.</p>
<p><b>Conclusion</b></p>
<p style="text-indent: 1em;">I think this book is enjoyable because it is simple, funny, and meaningful. It helped me understand that good friends are honest with each other and know how to cheer each other up.</p>
