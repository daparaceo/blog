<p><b>Introduction</b></p>
<p style="text-indent: 1em;">Zog, written by Julia Donaldson and illustrated by Axel Scheffler, tells the story of a young dragon who tries his hardest in dragon school every single year but always ends up hurt.</p>
<p><b>Body</b></p>
<p style="text-indent: 1em;">What I loved most about this book is how it gently turns a classic fairy-tale idea upside down. Instead of a princess waiting to be rescued, Princess Pearl says she would rather be a doctor than go back to the palace. Even the knight, Sir Gadabout, agrees that he would rather learn medicine than fight. The rhyming text also makes the story fun to read aloud, and the repeated line "What a good idea!" said Zog gives young readers a satisfying pattern to follow.</p>
<p><b>Conclusion</b></p>
<p style="text-indent: 1em;">Zog is a warm, funny story about trying your best, being kind to others, and choosing your own path in life, no matter what everyone expects of you.</p>
