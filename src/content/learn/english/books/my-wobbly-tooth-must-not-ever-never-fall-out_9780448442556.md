<div class="review-en">
  <p><b>Introduction</b></p>
  <p style="text-indent: 1em;"><i>My wobbly tooth must not ever never fall out</i> is a funny Charlie and Lola story by Lauren Child. It is about Lola’s first wobbly tooth and her very big feelings about it.</p>

  <p><b>Body</b></p>
  <p style="text-indent: 1em;">At first, Lola does not want her tooth to fall out at all. She thinks she needs to keep all of her teeth just the way they are. But when she hears about the tooth fairy, she becomes excited and changes her mind. I liked how Charlie helps Lola feel better when things do not go exactly as planned.</p>

  <p><b>Conclusion</b></p>
  <p style="text-indent: 1em;">This book is fun to read because Lola’s words are silly, dramatic, and sweet. It is a good story for children who are losing their baby teeth for the first time.</p>
