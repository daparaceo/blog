  <p><b>Introduction</b></p>
  <p style="text-indent: 1em;"><i>The magic hat</i> by Mem Fox is a funny and rhythmic story about a hat that brings magic to a town.</p>

  <p><b>Body</b></p>
  <p style="text-indent: 1em;">I liked this book because the words repeat in a playful way. The magic hat moves through the air and changes people into different animals. The pictures and rhymes make the story easy to imagine and fun to read aloud.</p>

  <p><b>Conclusion</b></p>
  <p style="text-indent: 1em;">I would recommend this book to readers who enjoy silly magic, animals, and repeating sentences. It is a good book for practicing rhythm in English.</p>
