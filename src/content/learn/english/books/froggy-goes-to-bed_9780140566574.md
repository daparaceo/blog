<p><b>Introduction</b></p>
<p style="text-indent: 1em;"><i>Froggy Goes to Bed</i> is a funny bedtime story by Jonathan London. It shows Froggy getting ready for bed, but he keeps finding one more thing to do.</p>

<p><b>Body</b></p>
<p style="text-indent: 1em;">I liked this book because Froggy is silly and full of energy. The repeated sounds, like "flop flop flop" and "splash splash splash," make the story fun to read aloud. The story also helps readers learn useful bedtime words such as bath, pajamas, toothbrush, snack, and story.</p>

<p><b>Conclusion</b></p>
<p style="text-indent: 1em;">This book is a good choice for young English learners because the story is simple, lively, and easy to connect with daily life. I would recommend it to children who enjoy funny picture books.</p>

<p style="text-indent: 1em;">이 책이 좋았던 이유는 Froggy가 엉뚱하고 에너지가 넘치기 때문입니다. "flop flop flop", "splash splash splash" 같은 반복되는 소리 표현은 이야기를 소리 내어 읽을 때 더 재미있게 만들어 줍니다. 또한 bath, pajamas, toothbrush, snack, story처럼 잠자리 준비와 관련된 유용한 단어를 배울 수 있습니다.</p>
