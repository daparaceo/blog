  <p style="text-indent: 1em;"><i>Goodnight Moon</i> by Margaret Wise Brown is a gentle bedtime story about a little bunny saying goodnight to everything nearby.</p>
  <p><b>Body</b></p>
  <p style="text-indent: 1em;">I liked the repeating phrase “Goodnight” and the rhyming words such as <i>moon</i> and <i>balloon</i>. The quiet rhythm made me imagine the room becoming darker and calmer. It also showed me that an ordinary room can feel warm and special when I pay attention to the things inside it.</p>
  <p><b>Conclusion</b></p>
  <p style="text-indent: 1em;">I think this is a comforting book to read aloud before bed. I would recommend it to young readers who enjoy rhyme, repetition, and peaceful pictures.</p>
